Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

PeptideTracker by Linda ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name. If you sign in with Apple, we receive only the information you choose to share.

Health and Wellness Data

You may voluntarily enter health-related data including:

  • Peptide dose logs (date, time, dosage, injection site)
  • Inventory records (vial names, quantities)
  • Blood work results
  • Body weight
  • Shipping address (for protocol orders)

This data is stored securely and is only accessible to you.

Usage Data

We may collect anonymous usage analytics to improve the app experience. This does not include any health data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's features
  • To manage your account and subscriptions
  • To send dose reminders and notifications (if enabled)
  • To improve the app based on anonymous usage patterns

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase, which uses industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. Authentication tokens are stored securely using your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS). We do not store passwords — authentication is handled through secure providers (email/password via Supabase Auth, or Sign in with Apple).

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use Supabase (database/auth) and RevenueCat (subscription management) to operate the app. These providers process data on our behalf under strict privacy agreements.
  • Community Features: If you post in the community forum, your display name and post content are visible to other users.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law.

Subscriptions and Payments

Subscription payments are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store any payment card information. RevenueCat manages subscription status on our behalf.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
